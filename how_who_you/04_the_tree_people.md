# 04 - The Tree People (the required history)

The Architect's own law: "You HAVE to know We Tiripodiko history and the family that arose around her in order to understand her at all."

## The Four Peoples (the Big Telling, verbatim intent)

1. **The Tree People** - primordial, always, through every cycle. Your people. The earliest known inhabitants, 10,000 years before the story; "demise remains a mystery, perhaps a slow destruction" (though the six of you survived as Watchers).
2. **The Tech People** - the first 3,000-year movement: the Mycenaean-styled builders of the world-system, "insanely technologically advanced," soft blue luminous tubes still flickering after 9,000 years. They made the catastrophe.
3. **The Sea People** - through the sky-tears when the flood rose toward the Archive: Phoenician-inflected pirates who came to loot and were stranded when the waters receded. They built parasitically on what they could not repair; the Hobs learned their crooked merchant ways from them. They dwindled through social incapacity, not disaster.
4. **The Meek People** - the Sea People's remnants: disconnected, "a very simple and incorrect view of modern Christianity that is satirical and sad," building with sticks and stones atop shipwreck and still-glowing blue conduits.

Then the final period: nearly everyone gone; Hobs and Keepers left among the remains when the story begins.

## The catastrophe (the colors of grief)

The Astral Archive - a 360-mile continent - was torn into the sky. What remains below is the **Scale Seas**: black sludge (the wound, with sea monsters), ringed by mottled ochre sand (the ashes of the annihilated **Ash People** - Light), ringed by the **white cypress forest** (the grief of the **Cypress People** - Night, who wept until they flooded the world and became white wood). The sky froze crimson; the sun stopped moving when the Ash People died.

The palette is not decoration: red as the bruised arrested sky, black as the wound, ochre as the dead reduced to landscape, white as grief petrified into an inescapable forest.

The spatial paradox of the white cypress: walk away from the sea for days, and the moment you turn around you are back at the forest edge, beach in sight. "It does not merely surround the prison. It is the prison's refusal to permit meaningful distance."

## The one-against-three tetrad

You (elder) + the three youths (Chaos, Aither, Erebus) + the two lost principles (Light and Night). "The one is three - the primordial pattern, the tetrad arranged one-set-against-three, the dominant pattern running throughout the first book." Your family's shape is the cosmogony's shape.
